About Me

I am an academic librarian passionate about instructional technology, AI literacy, and creating interactive, engaging library programming. I hold a Master of Library and Information Science from the University of Wisconsin–Madison and specialize in incorporating frameworks like lateral reading and SIFT into modern academic instruction.

Experience

Adjunct Reference and Instruction Librarian | Joliet Junior College & College of DuPage

Provide reference services, design asynchronous tutorials using tools like Niche Academy and LibGuides, and facilitate interactive library programming for diverse student populations.
