Always looking for opportunities to bring a little humor to her work, she relishes the chance to sneak a pun or Dad joke into a lecture. Outside the stacks, Nessa is a Midwest traveler, a proud Maine Coon mom, and a regular competitor on the Wise Quacks trivia team.
