About Me

As a librarian and educator, Nessa Vahedian uses a blend of creative pedagogy and professional competence to spark a genuine sense of curiosity in her students and patrons. She makes it her mission to bring the library to the community whenever possible, championing its role as an essential pillar of democracy and a cornerstone for intellectual freedom.

Always looking for opportunities to bring a little humor to her work, she relishes the chance to sneak a pun or Dad joke into a lecture. Outside the stacks, Nessa is a Midwest traveler, a proud Maine Coon mom, and a regular competitor on the Wise Quacks trivia team. To see her work in practice, feel free to explore her teaching materials and professional portfolio.
