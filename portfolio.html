Selected Instructional Projects

Behind the Chatbot: AI Literacy Module

An interactive, flipped-class approach utilizing an AI teaching assistant to guide students through the mechanics of large language models, including tokenization and vector embeddings. This project demonstrates ethical AI application and technical demystification for community college students.

Summer Fling Book Matchmaker

Designed an interactive library display utilizing Library of Congress classification matching mechanics to increase patron engagement. Supported by targeted signage and a social media campaign.

Banned Books Week Programming

Executed comprehensive thematic programming featuring clandestine book inserts, blackout poetry stations, and film screenings to highlight intellectual freedom.
